Formats

to run it.

Each is fully custom to your team's stack, codebase, and actual work. Pricing depends on cohort size and format — quoted after a 30-minute scoping call.

Single-day workshop

One day, in-person or remote. Intro to AI-native engineering and agentic workflows, live coding with Claude / Cursor / Copilot, hands-on exercises in your repo. Best for kicking off team-wide adoption.

1 DAY · UP TO 25 PEOPLE
FMT · 02

Two-week cohort

Eight 90-minute sessions over two weeks. Deeper dive: prompt engineering, agent design, evals, production patterns. Each session pairs theory with work-in-your-actual-codebase.

2 WEEKS · UP TO 15 PEOPLE

Quarterly residency

One of our senior engineers embeds with your team for a quarter — pair-programming, code reviews, weekly office hours. Best when you want the team to absorb AI practices by osmosis.

12 WEEKS · FULL TEAM
What we cover

Skills your team
will actually use.

Every cohort is customized, but most cover some combination of:

AI-native coding. Effective use of Claude, Cursor, Copilot, MCP — and where they break.
Prompt engineering that's not vibes. Patterns, anti-patterns, when to stop and switch tools.
Agent design. Building multi-step workflows that don't fall over on edge cases.
Evals & observability. How to know if your AI is actually working in production.
Cost & latency tradeoffs. Model selection, caching, batching, when to fine-tune.
Org patterns. How teams that ship AI well organize the work — RFCs, evals, on-call.
