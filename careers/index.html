Forward Deployed Engineer

Embed with clients. Ship agentic software that runs the business.

Remote / Hybrid Full-time Mid–Senior TypeScript · Python

As a Forward Deployed Engineer, you sit at the intersection of our clients and our codebase. You'll scope the real problem alongside a customer, design the agentic workflow, and ship a working solution — often within a single delivery cycle. This is a builder's role for someone who is equally comfortable writing production code and explaining a system to a non-technical stakeholder.

What you'll do

  • Embed directly with client teams to understand workflows and pain points
  • Design and build specialized AI agents that operate real software
  • Ship full-stack features end to end on tight, iterative cycles
  • Own delivery from kickoff to production and hand-off
  • Turn fuzzy requirements into a clear, working system

What we look for

  • Strong full-stack engineering (TypeScript/Node and/or Python)
  • Hands-on experience building with LLMs and agent frameworks
  • Comfort talking directly to customers and translating needs
  • A bias toward shipping and iterating over planning
  • Ownership mindset — you finish what you start

Senior Forward Deployed Engineer

Lead delivery on our most complex client engagements.

Remote / Hybrid Full-time Senior · Lead AI / Agents

A senior version of the role above — you'll take point on our most ambiguous, highest-stakes engagements. What you'll do

  • Lead end-to-end delivery on flagship client engagements

  • Mentor engineers and raise the technical bar across the team
  • Partner with client leadership on scope and roadmap
  • Define patterns we reuse across future engagements

What we look for

  • 5+ years shipping production software, recent AI/agent work
  • Track record owning delivery for external customers
  • Deep judgment on reliability, evals, and safe automation
  • Clear communicator who earns client trust quickly
  • Comfortable leading without a heavy process
