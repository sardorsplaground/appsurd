Sets the vision, keeps the team shipping.

Sardor founded Appsurd to fix what he saw breaking again and again inside fast-moving companies: software hiring that takes months, burns six figures, and still leaves a single point of failure. His answer was a productized squad — engineers, designers, and PMs on a flat monthly subscription, shipping every 48 to 72 hours.

He spends his time at the intersection of business operations and applied AI — designing the agentic workflows that let a small team deliver the output of a much larger one. When he writes here, expect strong opinions on hiring, automation, and the economics of building software in 2026.
