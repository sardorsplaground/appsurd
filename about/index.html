Our Story

We built the team we always wanted to hire.

Appsurd started as a question: why does assembling a great product squad take 3–6 months and $500k+ a year? We'd been building software since 2019 — 25+ brands, hundreds of features shipped. We knew the answer was AI. So we built it.

Origin

Our timeline

2019

Bolder Apps is founded.

We start shipping software for startups and enterprise brands — learning what it takes to build production-grade products fast, and what slows most teams down.

2023

AI changes everything.

We rebuild our entire workflow around agentic tools, LLMs, and AI-native engineers. Output per engineer climbs 3–5×. We don't just adopt AI — we redesign every process around it.

2026

Appsurd launches.

We package everything we learned into one product: a full squad, one price, 48-hour delivery. No bloated retainers. No contractor roulette. Just a team that ships.
